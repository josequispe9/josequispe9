## About Me

AI Engineer and Data Engineer. I design and build AI agents and data systems that solve real business problems: less manual work, better decisions and processes that scale.

I work across the whole stack, from data pipelines and modeling to deploying LLM agents (text and voice) in production and integrating them with the tools a business already uses.

- **Data engineering**: ETL/ELT pipelines, orchestration with **Apache Airflow**, data modeling on **BigQuery**
- **Analytics & BI**: metrics, dashboards and reports that drive decisions
